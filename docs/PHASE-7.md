# Phase 7 — Final Operational UI

Phase 7 is the final feature-building phase before full testing.

## Completed scope
- Internal navigation for all operational modules.
- Purchasing and supplier workflow screens.
- Inventory and stock movement workflow.
- Project material issue and cost visibility.
- Finance, VAT and profitability views.
- Print-friendly quotation and invoice document layouts.
- Settings for company details and default VAT.
- Responsive layout for desktop/tablet/mobile.
- Simple role model suitable for owners and workers; avoid enterprise HR/accounting complexity.

## Testing after Phase 7
Do not add new business features during the first test pass. Verify workflows end-to-end with realistic Dubai construction/fit-out scenarios.
